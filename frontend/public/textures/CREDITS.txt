Texture credits
===============

Planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth day/night/clouds, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn,
Uranus, Neptune), the Sun, Earth's Moon, the dwarf planets (Ceres, Eris,
Haumea, Makemake — "fictional" concept maps), and the Milky Way panorama
(milkyway.jpg):
  Solar System Scope — https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  Hero bodies are the 8K masters downscaled to 4K equirectangular.

Titan, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Enceladus:
  Steve Albers, from NASA mosaics (Voyager / Galileo / Cassini / Juno) —
  https://stevealbers.net/albers/sos/sos.html
  Derived from NASA imagery, which is in the public domain.

Callisto:
  Björn Jónsson (bjj.mmedia.is), from NASA/Voyager/Galileo data — public domain.

These are equirectangular surface maps wrapped onto the 3D bodies by the
universe engine. Bodies without a bundled map (Pluto, Charon, the small
moons, comet nuclei) render procedurally instead.

To add more bodies: drop an equirectangular map named <body>.jpg here and
reference it from frontend/lib/universe-data.ts. USGS global mosaics:
  https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search
